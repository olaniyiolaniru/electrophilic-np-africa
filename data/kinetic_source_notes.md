# Kinetic source verification

The 36 rates were checked against Table 2 of Böhme et al. (2009), DOI 10.1021/tx800492x, and Table 1 of Böhme et al. (2010), DOI 10.1021/tx100226n. The selection contains the 23 alkene-conjugated carbonyls from the 2009 kinetic table and the 13 alkene-conjugated aldehydes added in 2010. The two propiolate esters, one ynone, and two ynals are excluded because their reacting conjugated system contains a triple bond. Five further ester records collated in the 2010 paper originate outside the 2009 table and are not part of this defined 36-record calibration.

The response is log10[kGSH/(L mol^-1 min^-1)]. Rates in these units convert to M^-1 s^-1 by division by 60, shifting the log by -1.77815125. These are apparent second-order rates based on total GSH, not thiolate-normalized intrinsic rates. The 2009 kinetic protocol uses phosphate buffer pH 7.4 at 25 °C, 0.14 mM GSH, DMSO cosolvent, and DTNB detection at 412 nm; the 2010 work invokes that same chemoassay. Oxidative GSH loss is accounted for in the source for the marked low-reactivity compounds. Reported errors are transcribed without reinterpretation; the 2009 table identifies standard errors.

The source rate for trans-2-methyl-2-pentenal is 0.350, but the printed log is -0.56. The calibration computes all logarithms directly from reported rates, giving -0.455931956 for this record. Both source values and the adopted convention remain traceable. The 2-octen-4-one structure used has eight carbons, consistent with the source name. Explicit trans/cis names are encoded stereochemically. Unspecified source geometries remain unspecified.
